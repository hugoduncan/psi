❌ A workflow summary step whose prompt context includes the per-prompt review replies will echo their `PASS_STATUS:` lines into its own output, producing >1 `PASS_STATUS:` line. `parse-pass-status-routing` requires exactly one column-0 `PASS_STATUS: <TOKEN>` line and hard-fails with `:ambiguous-pass-status` on duplicates — crashing the lifecycle gate that consumes the summary. When authoring a routable summary template (task 229 DI-4): emit exactly one column-0 `PASS_STATUS: <TOKEN>` as the final line, and add an explicit anti-echo guard ("do not reproduce the status lines carried in the contributed review replies"). Do NOT phrase the anti-echo guard using the literal `PASS_STATUS:` token — the token in the instruction body itself counts toward the parser's single-occurrence rule. Keep the separate prose-body guard ("respond with a concise summary, not a control token"); the anti-echo and anti-control-token guards are distinct concerns, do not collapse them.
