🎯 A prompt/review guardrail that only records a deferred human decision is not a safety gate. In `task-lifecycle`, unchecked `SCOPE_QUESTION:` items could be filed and deferred while the lifecycle still proceeded to plan/implement/extract, silently defaulting scope. For psi workflows, any human-owned decision marker that affects scope or semantics needs a machine-checkable unresolved signal and an explicit workflow gate before autonomous execution. Prefer content-based scanning of the authoritative task artifact over reviewer convergence/status, because a review can converge after deferring the item. Resume should be stateless/idempotent: the human records the rationale, flips the machine signal, and re-running the workflow rescans current state.
