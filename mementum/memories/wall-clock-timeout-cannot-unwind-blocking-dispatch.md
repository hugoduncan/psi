Correct teardown for a bounded helper run: make the *run future itself* own its cleanup — it closes + untracks the child in its own `finally`, once the uninterruptible blocking call actually returns/throws. The timeout branch returns promptly (`:text nil` → no-op) but leaves the child **tracked** (recursion-safe) and unclosed until that finally fires. Do NOT call `future-cancel` (can't unwind the call; cancel-then-deref also defeats genuine-settlement detection).
