Put the canonical predicate + `visible-*`/`hidden-*` partition at the LOWEST shared component both callers can reach: here `psi.skill-registry.registry` (tui cannot depend on prompt-assets), with prompt-assets aliasing it. Route every call-site through it.

Cross-surface boolean frontmatter coercion (skill SKILL.md vs markdown-workflow frontmatter) should share one helper (`pt/frontmatter-flag` in prompt-templates), one normalization rule (literal `true`/`false`, case-insensitive, trimmed; absent → default).
