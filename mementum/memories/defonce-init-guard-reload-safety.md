🔁 Extension `init` guard atoms must use `defonce`, not `def`. Without `defonce`, namespace reload creates a fresh atom, defeating the CAS-based reload safety guarantee and allowing duplicate handler registration. Precedent: `auto-session-name` uses `defonce ^:private`; `context-manager` initially used `def` and was caught by code-shaper review. Apply `defonce` to any extension-level atom that guards against re-initialization or tracks registration state.
