- 2026-05-04 resolution: first-cut delegate steps do not re-export callee step-local outputs. A delegate step yields the delegated workflow's yielded value unchanged, and downstream `{:step ... :output ...}` refs against the delegate step are invalid unless the delegate step itself declares explicit local outputs in a later extension. Aligned `design.md`, `doc/workflow-ir.md`, `doc/workflow-grammar-concepts.md`, and loosened `doc/workflow-grammar.md` output-key grammar back to `keyword` so per-step-type validation, rather than the authoring grammar, owns the allowed output surface set.
